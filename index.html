THE COACH

MEET ISAAC DUNCAN

TRAIN WITH EXPERIENCE.

Whether you're stepping into a boxing gym for the first time or looking to take your skills further, I provide focused coaching built around you and your goals.

I'm an England Boxing Level 2 Coach, Yorkshire Development Champion and Personal Trainer based at the Ministry of Boxing in Leeds.

I've worked with people of all ages, abilities and levels of experience. Whether you're a complete beginner looking to build confidence and fitness, or an experienced boxer looking to sharpen your skills, I'll tailor every session to you and help you get the most out of your training.
